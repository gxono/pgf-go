		\end{goban}

	\medskip
	The white stone \pgfgostone[w, mark = triangle] has three black neighbours,
	\pgfgoview{before}{B3, C2, C4}.
	Black plays 1 \pgfgoview{capture}{D3} and takes it:
	now the shape is \pgfgoview{capture}{B2:E4}. White cannot take back at once, because the
	same position would come again. White plays a threat first, 2 \pgfgoview{retake}{A5},
	Black answers with 3 \pgfgoview{retake}{A4}, and only then White retakes with 4
	\pgfgoview{retake}{C3}: \pgfgoview{retake}{B2:E4}.
